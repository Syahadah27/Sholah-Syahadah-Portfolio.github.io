BIOLOGY · UNIVERSITAS INDONESIA

I’m someone who enjoys learning, exploring new ideas, and finding ways to connect different interests.

With a background in Biology from Universitas Indonesia, much of my experience has been in conservation, biodiversity research, and environmental education. Through fieldwork and community programs, I’ve come to appreciate both careful observation and the people behind meaningful environmental work.

Lately, I’ve also been exploring coding, web development, and data analysis. I started learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build this portfolio, while continuing to learn tools such as Python, SQL, and Power BI. I’m curious about how these skills can help me approach problems, work with information, and create something useful.

This website is a collection of my experiences, projects, and ongoing learning as I continue to figure out where my interests and skills can take me.
